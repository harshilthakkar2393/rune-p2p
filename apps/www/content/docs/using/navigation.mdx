---
title: Home, Pages, Folders, Search
description: Time, space, and find. What each view is for.
---

Four ways to get to a page, each answering a different question.

## Home: what was I doing?

What you opened recently, grouped by day. Recents sync, so the phone knows what you opened on the Mac. Home is the start screen and the place to pick up where you left off.

## Pages: show me everything

Every page, newest first. Filter by when it was updated, sort by created or updated, select several to move or trash them. This is the whole space in one grid.

Opening a folder from the sidebar, or picking one in the filter, scopes this same view to that folder. The header becomes the folder: its colour, its name, and a menu to rename, recolour or trash it.

## Folders: where does this belong?

Folders are flat, coloured, and mandatory: every page is in exactly one. Think of them as areas of your life, not directories. On the Mac they live in the sidebar. On the phone the **Folders** tab in the dock shows them as tiles, with **All pages** first.

## Search: I know its name

⌘K on a keyboard. The magnifier in the dock on a phone, where the field sits at the bottom, on the keyboard, and shows what you opened recently until you type. Search matches page titles and folder names. Enter opens the first result.

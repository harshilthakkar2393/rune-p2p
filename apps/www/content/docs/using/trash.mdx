---
title: Trash
description: Thirty days to change your mind. Then it's gone from every device.
---

Trashing a page or a folder doesn't delete it. It moves it to Trash, where it stays for **30 days**. Trashing a folder trashes its pages with it.

## Restore

Open Trash, select what you want back, press **Restore**. A page returns to its folder; if that folder was trashed too, restore the folder first.

## Delete forever

**Delete forever** removes the page's content from this device now. The next time a paired device connects, it removes its copy too. This is the one action in Rune that cannot be undone anywhere.

## After 30 days

Rune deletes expired items automatically when the app opens. The 30 days count from when the item was trashed, on whichever device did it; the timestamp syncs with everything else.

## How deletion syncs

A trashed page is still a page, just flagged. That flag syncs like any edit, so the page appears in Trash on every device. Permanent deletion syncs as a tombstone: the index remembers that the page existed and is gone, so a device that was offline knows to drop its content rather than resurrect it.

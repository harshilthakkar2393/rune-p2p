title: Your first page
description: Folders, pages and the editor in one screen.
---

Rune has two kinds of things: **folders** and **pages**. Every page lives in exactly one folder. Folders don't nest.

## Make a page

Press **New page** in the sidebar, or the pencil button in the phone dock, or ⌘N. The page opens in the editor with the title focused. It goes into the folder you last used; you can move it later.

## Write

The editor is block based. Type to write, press Enter for a new block. Type `/` for the menu of block types: headings, lists, quotes, code, checklists. Drag the handle on the left of a block to move it. Select across blocks and the formatting bar appears.

There is no save button. Every keystroke is stored as you type, on this device, and sent to any paired device that is currently open.

## Organise

The sidebar lists your folders. Press **+** beside **Folders** to add one, give it a name and a colour. Open a folder to see its pages. Drag is not a thing here; to move a page, select it and use **Move** in the bar that appears, or open the page and change its folder in the inspector.

## Find things again

- **Home** shows what you opened recently, grouped by day.
- **Pages** shows everything, newest first, with a date filter.
- **Search** finds pages by title and folders by name. ⌘K on a keyboard, the magnifier in the dock on a phone.

That's the whole app. The rest of these docs is about how it keeps your notes and moves them between devices.

---
title: What is Rune
description: Private notes that live on your devices and sync to each other directly.
---

Rune is a notes app for one person. Every page you write is stored on the device you wrote it on, and on every other device you have paired. There is no account to create, no server that holds a copy, and no company that can read anything.

It is a **tool, not an agent**. It never appears uninvited, never watches the clipboard, never suggests. You open it, you write, you close it.

## What it is

- A page editor in the Notion style: blocks, slash menu, drag to reorder.
- Folders to keep pages apart, and a Pages view to see everything at once.
- Sync between your own devices over a direct, encrypted connection. Same Wi-Fi, the traffic never leaves your router.
- An installable web app. On iPhone it lives on the home screen, opens offline, and behaves like an app.

## What it is not

- **Not a cloud.** Nothing you write is uploaded anywhere. If all your devices are gone, so are your notes, unless you exported them. That trade is deliberate; see [Where your notes live](/docs/how-it-works/where-notes-live).
- **Not collaborative.** One person, several devices. The architecture could carry more, and there is no interface for it on purpose.
- **Not smart.** No AI, no reminders, no "you might also like".

## How to read these docs

If you only read one section, read [How it works](/docs/how-it-works/where-notes-live). It explains the one unusual thing about Rune: no account, no server, and it still syncs. Everything else follows from that.
